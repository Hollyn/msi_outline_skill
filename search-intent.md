Search engines have spent enormous effort modelling why someone searches a given phrase and what they expect back. Matching that expectation is a precondition for ranking, not an optimisation on top of it.

Read all three dimensions off the live SERP. The SERP is the answer key.

## Content type

Four broad categories: blog post, category page, product page, landing/home page.

Search "Nike running shoes" and the results are category and product pages from e-commerce sites — the intent is transactional. Search "potato pancakes" and the results are recipe blog posts — the intent is informational.

**This one is a hard gate.** If the SERP for your keyword returns predominantly product or category pages, a blog post will not rank for it, no matter how well written. Flag it and stop rather than producing an unusable brief.

Underlying intent categories worth naming: informational (most blog posts), navigational, commercial investigation (comparing, nearly ready to buy), and transactional.

## Content format

How the content is packaged: how-to guide, step-by-step tutorial, listicle, opinion piece, review, comparison, and so on.

Read it off the titles. If "best email marketing tools" returns "28 best", "7 best", "16 best" — the SERP wants a listicle and nothing else will fit. If "blogging for profit" returns a mix of "how to start" guides and "15 ways" listicles, either works, and a guide-shaped listicle may fit best.

## Content angle

The hook the searcher is selecting for — visible in the recurring words across ranking titles.

For "SEO tactics": *traffic-generating*, *drive organic traffic*, *increase organic traffic*, *new techniques*. Two angles are doing the work — **more traffic** and **new or unique**.

For "tent for winter camping": *best*, detailed reviews, and *insulated* appearing repeatedly. The angle is insulation, and the format is a detailed review roundup.

Combine the three into a working title before outlining. "How to start" (angle: beginners) plus listicle plus guide gives you: *a 15-step guide for new bloggers*.

## Ranking on authority vs ranking on merit

A page can rank while breaking every structural rule if it has enough backlinks. When a top result has no heading structure and hundreds of referring domains, that is what you are seeing.

Do not copy its structure. Note it in the brief, and take structural signal from the pages ranking without that backlink advantage.
