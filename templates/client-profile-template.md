# Client profile: <client name>

Fill this in once per client. It gets reused for every outline after that, so it
is worth ten minutes now.

Short answers are fine. The only field that really needs thought is "What they
know that competitors don't" — that one decides whether the outline says
something new or just reorganises what is already ranking.

Delete the italic prompts as you go.

---

**Site:**
*The domain.*

**Country:**
*Where the audience searches from. US unless it isn't.*

**What they sell:**
*One line. The actual paid offer, not the mission.*

**Default CTA:**
*Where posts should send people. Book a consultation, start a trial, request a
quote. If it changes by topic, say so.*

---

## Who they serve

*Be specific. "Parents" is not usable. "Parents of 3 to 6 year olds thinking
about pulling their child out of a traditional school mid-year" is. The
narrower this is, the better the angles that come out of it.*

## What they know that competitors don't

*This is the important one.*

*The pages currently ranking for these keywords are mostly written by
generalists. What does this client know that those writers do not? Years in the
job, a pattern they see over and over with customers, their own data, a
position most of the industry gets wrong.*

*Two or three specific things beat a paragraph of positioning.*

*Example of a weak answer: "We really care about quality and personal service."*

*Example of a strong answer: "The founder taught in both systems for 11 years
and thinks the transition year is where most families fail. Most articles treat
the choice as permanent, and it isn't."*

## Things they will not say

*Claims they can't make, competitors they won't name, legal or professional
limits, topics they consider settled. Anything that would cause a rewrite if it
showed up in a draft.*

## Voice

*Only what changes an outline. Reading level, whether first person is allowed,
whether they use humour. The writers already have the full style guide, so keep
this short.*

## Internal links

*Main pages new posts should link back to. Service pages, cornerstone guides.*

## Anything else

*Anything a new writer would need to be told in their first week on this
account.*
